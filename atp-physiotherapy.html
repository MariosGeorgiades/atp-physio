What We Offer

From traditional physiotherapy to holistic healing modalities — a full spectrum of care tailored to your recovery.

Sports Rehabilitation

Targeted programs to recover from sports injuries and return athletes to peak performance safely and efficiently.

Cupping Therapy

Ancient technique using suction cups to improve blood flow, relieve muscle tension, promote healing, and reduce inflammation.

Acupuncture

Precise needle therapy targeting key points to alleviate pain, reduce stress, and restore the body's natural energy flow.

Therapeutic Massage

Deep tissue and sports massage techniques to release tension, improve circulation, and accelerate muscle recovery.

Electrotherapy

Advanced modalities including TENS, ultrasound, and laser therapy to accelerate healing and manage chronic pain.

Manual Therapy

Hands-on joint mobilization, soft tissue manipulation, and myofascial release for pain relief and improved mobility.

Orthopedic Therapy

Expert treatment for musculoskeletal injuries, post-surgical recovery, joint dysfunction, and chronic conditions.

Dry Needling

Trigger point therapy using fine needles to release muscle knots, reduce referred pain, and restore movement patterns.

About ATP

Built on Passion for Movement

ATP Physiotherapy is a dedicated physiotherapy center blending modern clinical practice with holistic healing. Our mission is simple: your needs are our goal.

We combine evidence-based physiotherapy with time-tested techniques like cupping and acupuncture, creating comprehensive treatment plans that address the whole person — not just the injury.
